## 1\. Start Here — Who Holds Your Data

Before you make a request, it's worth checking you're asking the right organisation, because it will get you a faster answer.

**If you dealt with Consiva directly** — you visited consiva.ai, enquired, requested a walkthrough, or use the platform as a customer or an Authorised User — **we are the right party.** Continue below.

**If a business you dealt with uses Consiva** — you saw a consent banner powered by Consiva, or a business told you it manages consent through Consiva — **that business is the right party, not us.** They decided what data to collect and why; they hold the relationship and the obligation. We process it on their instructions and cannot act on your request in respect of their data. Contact them directly. If you can't identify them or find their contact route, write to us and we will do what we reasonably can to point you in the right direction.

## 2\. What You Can Ask Us For

**Access** — a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we are doing with it, and the identities of others with whom we have shared it.

**Correction, completion and updating** — to have inaccurate or misleading data corrected, and incomplete data completed.

**Erasure** — to have your personal data erased, where it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for and no law requires us to keep it.

**Withdraw consent** — where our processing of your data relies on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and does not affect processing that rests on another lawful ground — for example, we must keep invoice records for tax purposes even if you withdraw marketing consent.

**Nominate someone** — nominate another individual to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

**Raise a grievance** — about how we have handled your data or your request. See Section 6.

## 4\. Verifying Who You Are

We will verify your identity before acting, because disclosing personal data to someone who is not you would itself be a breach.

Verification is usually straightforward — confirming you control the email address associated with the data. Where a request is broader or higher risk, we may ask for further information. We will ask for the minimum necessary, and we will not require you to create an account to make a request.

If we cannot verify you, we will tell you why, and what would allow us to proceed.

## 5\. Our Response

**Acknowledgement:** we will acknowledge your request within **3 business days**.

**Substantive response:** within **30 days** of verifying your identity.

**No charge.** We do not charge for exercising your rights.

**If we cannot fully comply**, we will tell you which part we cannot action and why — for example where a statutory retention obligation prevents erasure, where the data is needed to establish or defend a legal claim, or where complying would disclose another person's personal data.

**Reasonable limits.** We may ask for clarification where a request is unclear, and we may decline to act on repetitive or manifestly excessive requests — but we will explain our reasoning rather than simply not responding.
