## Why is pricing so hard to find?

Three reasons, and it is worth knowing which one you are dealing with. **Enterprise sales models** — global platforms price on modules, data volume, entity count and negotiation, so publishing a number would anchor every deal. **Services-led vendors** — where the software is a wrapper around a consulting engagement, the price is the engagement, and that genuinely varies. **Positioning** — a vendor selling to CMOs on a trust narrative would rather discuss business risk than a monthly figure.

None of these is illegitimate. But it makes budgeting hard, and it is why a category with a 2027 deadline still has buyers who cannot get a range.

The cookie-only row is where most Indian buyers start and where most get stuck. A cookie banner is one obligation out of roughly a dozen. It does nothing for rights requests, retention, breach reporting or records.

## The seven cost drivers that actually move the number

Whoever you buy from, these are what the quote turns on. **Consent volume** — usually the primary meter, and vendors define the unit differently. **Domain or property count** — multi-brand businesses get expensive fast. **Whether Data Principal rights are metered** — ask this explicitly; rights requests are unmetered on every Consiva plan. **Data discovery and ROPA** — usually a premium tier, and the capability most likely to justify the spend. **Language coverage** — DPDP notices must be available in any of the 22 Eighth Schedule languages at the individual's option. **Implementation and onboarding** — sometimes bundled, sometimes a separate line item that exceeds the licence in year one. **Deployment model** — on-premise or single-tenant deployments typically carry a substantial premium.

## Questions worth asking before you sign

- What exactly counts as one billable consent? Does a rejection count? Does a returning visitor re-consenting count as new?
- Are Data Principal rights requests capped or charged per request?
- What is the per-additional-domain price?
- How many Eighth Schedule languages are live today?
- Is there an uptime commitment, and is it in the contract or on the website?
- Will you sign a Data Processing Agreement, and can I see your subprocessor list with processing locations?
- **Are you a registered Consent Manager under Rule 4?** Before 13 November 2026 the honest answer is no, because registration isn't open. → [A vendor that says yes has told you something useful](https://consiva.ai/blog/consent-manager-vs-cmp)
- What happens to my consent records if I leave?

## Why won't vendors publish pricing?+

Enterprise module-and-volume pricing models, services-led engagements that genuinely vary, and a preference for discussing risk rather than a figure. It makes budgeting harder than it needs to be.

Should Data Principal rights requests be charged per request?+

We don't think so. Responding is an unconditional statutory obligation with a response deadline, so metering it prices the point at which a customer starts breaching the law. Ask any vendor this directly.

What's the biggest hidden cost?+

Usually implementation, or the per-additional-domain rate for multi-brand businesses. Get both in writing before signing.
