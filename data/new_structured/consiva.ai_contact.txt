## Contact & Support FAQs

### How Fast Can We Expect to Hear Back From the Consiva Team?

Our compliance team is available to handle all your questions Monday through Friday from 10am to 5pm IST. But don't worry if you need some help outside those hours — our Pro and Enterprise customers have access to 24/7 priority support to get things sorted out quickly.

### Are the Free Compliance Scans Really No-Cost?

You bet — our free DPDP scan for your website is completely free. We'll analyze your cookie setup, terms of service, how you're collecting data, and make sure you're ready for CERT-In. And the best part? You get a detailed report with some practical steps to take care of any gaps — all for free.

### What to Have Prepared Before We Chat About a Demo with You ?

To get the best from a demo with us you'll want to make sure you've got a few things lined up beforehand. First off, have a rough idea of how many visitors your website gets each month - this will help us get on the same page. You should also think about how many domains you're hosting, and whether you've got a clear handle on your current privacy policy situation. And if you've already dabbled with compliance tools, it's worth knowing what you've got in place. That way we can shape the demo to your business and make sure you get the most out of it.

### Does Consiva Handle Enterprise Deployments?

We sure do. Our Enterprise plan comes with all the bells and whistles — a dedicated account manager to keep things running smoothly, custom integrations to fit your workflow, SLA guarantees so you know what to expect, and an India-based compliance expert to help with DPDP gap analysis and CERT-In readiness.
