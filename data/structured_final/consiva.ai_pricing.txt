## Pricing FAQ

## Common Pricing Questions

### Is there a free trial for paid plans?

The Pro plan doesn't include a time-limited trial. Instead, our Free plan is permanently free with no credit card required — use it for as long as you need to evaluate the platform, then upgrade to Pro whenever you're ready for higher limits and full automation.

### What happens if my consent limit gets exceeded?

We'll shoot you an alert when you hit 80% and then again at 95% usage. Once you've gone over your limit, that consent banner just keeps on showing up (we don't let compliance fall through the gaps) but we do pause any recording you're doing until you either upgrade or add a Cookie Pack to your account.

### Can I just cancel my subscription whenever I feel like it?

You can, yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time & you get to keep using the service til the end of your current billing cycle. Annual plans are a no-refund deal after 30 days, but if you do cancel before your renewal date you should be good.

### Do you offer any breaks for NGOs or startups?

We do — we're giving registered Indian NGOs and DPIIT-recognized startups a 30% discount. If you want to claim your discount just send us your registration certificate to [support@consiva.ai](mailto:support@consiva.ai).

### Is the annual discount applied automatically?

Yes. Toggle the billing switch above to Annual and the discounted price is applied immediately at checkout. Annual billing is charged as a single upfront payment.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, and bank transfer (for Enterprise plans). GST invoices are issued for all Indian customers. International cards accepted for global customers.
