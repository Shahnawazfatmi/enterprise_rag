## Our Story

## The First Platform Built Natively for DPDP — Not Retrofitted from GDPR

When India passed the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023** in August 2023, most Indian businesses turned to GDPR-focused compliance tools from Europe and tried to adapt them. The result was compliance gaps, incorrect consent models, and tools that ignored India-specific obligations like CERT-In's 6-hour breach reporting.

We saw the problem clearly: **Indian businesses needed an Indian solution**. Consiva was built from the ground up for the DPDP Act — using India's legal framework, India's language requirements, and India's infrastructure. Not a GDPR tool with an India label pasted on top.

Today, Consiva.ai powers consent management, breach detection, and Data Principal Rights workflows for over 500 Indian businesses — from Bangalore startups to Mumbai enterprises. Every feature is designed around the specific text of the DPDP Act 2023.

## Why Consiva

## Built Different — Built for India

Four things that make Consiva categorically different from any other compliance tool on the market.

### DPDP-Native Architecture

Consiva's consent model was designed from Section 1 of the DPDP Act 2023 — not adapted from GDPR. Every feature reflects how Indian law defines consent (free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous), not how GDPR defines it. No legitimate-interest workaround. No mislabelled consent bases. Just DPDP compliance done right.

India-first design

### Dual CERT-In Breach Tracker

When an incident is logged, Consiva simultaneously starts a 6-hour CERT-In countdown and a DPDP Board notification workflow — with auto-generated report drafts for both agencies. This dual-tracker is unique to Consiva and was built specifically for India's overlapping regulatory obligations from the Ministry of Electronics and CERT-In.

CERT-In 6-hour rule covered

### India Data Residency

All personal data processed by Consiva — consent records, breach logs, Data Principal Rights requests — is stored exclusively on servers located in India. Our data centres are ISO 27001 certified. For enterprise customers, we can deploy within your own private cloud on Indian infrastructure to meet sensitive data obligations.
