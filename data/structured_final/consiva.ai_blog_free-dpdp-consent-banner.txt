## What's not included

Being straightforward about this, because a free plan that hides its limits wastes your time.

- **One domain only.** Additional domains need Pro.
- **English and Hindi only.** DPDP notices must be available in English or any of the 22 Eighth Schedule languages at the individual's option — full language coverage is on Pro.
- **Basic scanning, not scheduled.** You trigger scans; Pro runs them automatically and alerts on new trackers.
- **No breach module.** The CERT-In and DPDP breach clock tracking is on Pro.
- **No automated DSR workflows.** Manual handling on free.
- **No parental consent workflow.** Section 9 verifiable parental consent is on Pro.
- **No Data Discovery or ROPA.** Database scanning and records of processing are Enterprise.
- **No uptime SLA.** We don't publish one on any plan — we run from a single Indian data centre and won't promise a figure we can't stand behind contractually.

## Is a free banner enough for you?

Honest answer: it depends on three things. **Volume** — 1,000 cookie consents a month suits roughly a few thousand monthly visitors, depending on your return-visitor ratio. **Scope** — if you run one website, collect leads through a form or two, and use analytics plus maybe one advertising pixel, the free plan covers your consent obligation properly. **Obligation breadth** — cookie consent is one obligation out of roughly a dozen. DPDP also requires Rule 3-compliant notice content, Data Principal rights fulfilment, retention and erasure logic, breach intimation, a published grievance contact and records.

**Where the free plan is genuinely the right answer:** a single marketing site or blog; an early-stage startup with modest traffic; a business that wants consent capture live now and will scale later; or anyone who wants to run a real scan and see what's actually firing on their site before committing budget. → [Run that check in 10 minutes](https://consiva.ai/blog/tags-firing-before-consent)

**Where it isn't:** multiple domains or storefronts; a database that needs discovery and a ROPA; regulated-sector retention conflicts; a user base including under-18s; or anyone above a Third Schedule threshold with Rule 8 erasure clocks to run.
