## Response Times

Business hours are **10:00 AM – 5:00 PM IST, Monday to Friday**, excluding public holidays.

## | Enquiry | Target |
| --- | --- |

| Privacy rights request — acknowledgement | 3 business days |

Our 30-day rights and grievance targets are **Consiva's service commitment**. Rule 14(3) of the DPDP Rules sets a ceiling of a reasonable period not exceeding ninety days, so our target sits well inside the statutory limit — deliberately.
